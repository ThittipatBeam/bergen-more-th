# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Thai AI developers, machine learning engineers, and RAG practitioners evaluating LLM models, embedding retrievers, and rerankers for Thai-language document retrieval and question answering.

## Product Purpose

Provide an open, transparent, and reproducible benchmark of Thai RAG pipelines. Scored on Thai Wikipedia QA by an independent LLM judge (Kimi K3) over 500 QA pairs, giving practitioners clarity on the optimal combination of retriever, reranker, and generator for Thai text.

## Positioning

The definitive empirical benchmark for Thai RAG architectures. Grounded in real Thai text nuances rather than translated English benchmarks, sponsored by SIAM.AI Cloud.

## Operating Context

Visitors scan the benchmark to compare pipeline combinations, identify top performers for their production budgets, check latency vs. accuracy trade-offs, and inspect exact metrics across models.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Interactive leaderboard table with sorting and filtering by generator model and reranker presence.
- Stacked hero champion cards featuring top models across accuracy, latency, and Thai lexical overlap.
- Accessible color contrast complying with WCAG AA standards.
- Dark and light theme support.

## Brand Commitments

- Clean, high-density scientific presentation.
- SIAM.AI Cloudy Sky palette: crisp blues, cyans, and clean white/dark surfaces.
- Fast, responsive, mobile-first design.
